Abstract

This meta-analysis synthesizes performance data from 142 discrete solar cell prototypes developed between 2021 and 2024. Our findings suggest that while laboratory-controlled efficiency has peaked at 29.8%, field performance in high-humidity tropical environments continues to lag due to degradation of the perovskite lattice structure. We provide a detailed comparison of encapsulation techniques across different latitudes and propose a standardized framework for reporting regional environmental variables in future publications.
